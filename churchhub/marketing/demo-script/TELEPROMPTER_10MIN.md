# ChurchHub — 10-Minute Teleprompter Script

**How to use:** Load in teleprompter software at large font, high contrast.  
Read only the spoken lines. Lines in `[brackets]` are silent action cues — do not read aloud.  
Pace: calm, ~120–140 words/minute. Pause one beat on each `[PAUSE]`.

**Duration:** 10:00  
**Source:** `DEMO_SCRIPT_10MIN.md`

---

## 00:00  INTRODUCTION

[HOLD brand or login screen — do not type yet]

Good morning.

In the next ten minutes,
I’ll show you ChurchHub —
the church management system built for multi-level administration —
from the local church,
to the conference,
and beyond.

We’ll walk the path your teams use every week —
membership,
visitors,
giving,
treasury,
reports,
and meetings —

and we’ll close with how the platform
is operated safely across many churches.

Everything you’re about to see
respects roles,
church scope,
and audit.

Pastors see pastoral work.
Treasurers see the books.
Executives see the roll-up.

[CLICK into Staff Login]
[MOVE cursor to username]

---

## 00:30  LOGIN

[TYPE username]
[TAB]
[TYPE password]

Staff sign in through ChurchHub’s secure session login —
the same discipline you’d expect
for financial and membership data.

[CLICK Sign in]
[PAUSE — wait for dashboard]

No separate apps for clerk versus treasurer.
One workspace.
Permissions decide what appears.

[HOVER church switcher — do not change]

We’re in Demo Central Church,
under a conference with multiple congregations —
so the hierarchy is real —
not a flat, single-church toy.

[MOVE cursor to KPI strip]

This landing page is Mission Control.

---

## 01:00  DASHBOARD

[PAN slowly across KPI cards]

Executives and pastors get the pulse immediately —
giving this month,
remittance position,
membership shape —
scoped to the church you’re working in.

[SCROLL to Action Queue]
[HOVER two or three queue items — do not click]

The Action Queue is the pastor and admin to-do list —
open visitors,
pending transfers,
financial approvals
waiting for a second pair of eyes.

[SCROLL to teller section or finance trend]
[HOVER chart bars]

Treasurers see the business date
and teller activity —
so Sabbath posting isn’t guesswork.

Trends show income versus expense
over recent months.

[HOVER This Week Pulse chips if visible]

Pastoral care signals sit in the same place —
birthdays,
meetings,
follow-ups —
without hunting five menus.

[CLICK Members → Directory]

Let’s start where ministry starts —
people.

---

## 02:00  MEMBERSHIP

[CLICK search]
[TYPE two or three letters of a demo surname]

Clerks and pastors search a living directory.
Filter by status,
church,
and more —
always limited to churches
you’re authorized to see.

[CLICK a well-populated member]
[SCROLL profile slowly]

A member profile is the single record —
identity,
membership status,
family,
church placement —
not a spreadsheet tab per department.

[OPEN Journey / timeline — or scroll history]
[HOVER baptism or transfer event]

The journey timeline preserves history —
baptism,
transfer,
leadership —
so we never overwrite someone’s story.

[HOVER Families / Leadership / Transfers in nav]

The same module covers families,
leadership assignments,
baptisms,
and transfers —
with a full audit trail.

[CLICK Visitors]

Guests become discipleship opportunities.

---

## 03:00  VISITORS

[POINT at open follow-ups]
[CLICK one open visitor]

Pastors and greeters capture who visited,
who invited them,
and what’s next —
so Sabbath guests don’t disappear by Tuesday.

[SCROLL interests / follow-up / assigned elder]

Follow-up is assignable —
and it shows up in the Action Queue
we saw on the dashboard.

[CLICK Convert to member]
[SHOW form — do not submit]
[HOVER mapped fields]

When they’re ready,
conversion carries visit history into membership —
continuity,
instead of re-typing.

[CANCEL or go back]
[OPEN Giving Center]

Stewardship next —
how we honor giving
with privacy and clarity.

---

## 04:00  GIVING

[SEARCH / select the same demo member]
[SHOW recent giving summary]

Giving lookup is permission-controlled.
Treasurers and authorized stewards
see contribution history.
Casual users don’t.

[OPEN Individual Giving Statement]
[SCROLL totals]
[HOVER print or export — do not wait on download]

Members and finance teams get a clear statement —
tithe,
offerings,
period totals —
suitable for counseling
or year-end conversations.

[HOVER Remittance / Cut-off in nav — do not open]

At conference level,
remittance and monthly cut-off
roll local faithfulness
into organizational accountability.

[CLICK Record Receipt]

Now the Sabbath desk —
recording the offering.

---

## 05:00  TREASURY

[SELECT member or anonymous as appropriate]
[ENTER a small demo amount]
[CHOOSE tithe or offering category]
[CONFIRM business date is visible]

Tellers post against the church’s business date —
not whatever the laptop clock says.

Categories and funds stay consistent
with your chart.

[CLICK Save / Post]
[LAND on Receipt Confirmation]
[OR open a pre-posted receipt and say the alternate line below]

Every posting lands on a confirmation
you can print or file —
reference,
amount,
method,
status.

[ALTERNATE if not posting live:]
I’ve opened a receipt we posted earlier this morning.

[HOVER print actions]
[POINT at approved or pending status]

Sensitive changes use maker-checker where configured —
one person records,
another approves —
so the books stay trustworthy.

[OPEN Transaction Register]
[FILTER today or this week]

The register is searchable,
church-scoped,
and auditable.

[CLICK Reports]

Leaders don’t live in journals.
They live in reports.

---

## 06:00  REPORTS

[SCROLL report catalog]
[HOVER member and finance report groups]

One report center —
membership,
attendance,
stewardship,
and accounting —
filtered by what your role may run.

[OPEN Tithe and Offering — or Financial Summary]
[SET date range to this month]
[RUN report]
[PAN totals]

Pastors and executives can answer,
“How are we doing in stewardship?”
in seconds —
by church,
period,
and the scope you already selected.

[HOVER CSV / Excel / PDF export]

Exports are ready for board packs —
still permission-aware,
still tenant-scoped.

[POINT at Trial Balance / Income Statement]

For treasurers and auditors —
trial balance,
income statement,
balance sheet —
double-entry integrity,
not a second set of books.

[CLICK Calendar or Meetings]

Ministry rhythm —
calendar and meetings.

---

## 07:00  MEETINGS AND CHURCH LIFE

[POINT at meetings, birthdays, announcements on calendar]

Church Life pulls the week together —
meetings,
birthdays,
communications —
so the pastoral team shares one calendar truth.

[CLICK an upcoming meeting]
[SCROLL agenda, minutes, decisions, actions]
[HOVER attendance if present]

Board and committee work
isn’t email archaeology.

Agenda,
minutes,
decisions,
and attendance
live on the meeting record —
with approval flow for minutes
when you need governance.

[OPEN Announcements]
[OPEN one published announcement]

Announcements publish to the church —
and the member portal can surface
the same message to members
on their phones.

[SWITCH to Platform tab — pre-logged operator]

Finally —
how ChurchHub itself is run
for many denominations and churches.

---

## 08:00  PLATFORM ADMINISTRATION

[SHOW Control Room]
[PAN operator widgets]

Platform Administration
is a separate lane from the church workspace.

ChurchHub operators manage the SaaS wall —
denominations,
tenants,
and health.

[OPEN Denominations or Subscriptions]
[HOVER plans and subscriptions]

Each denomination is isolated.

Subscriptions and plans control
what a tenant may use —
payroll,
assets,
advanced ledger —
without mixing data across the wall.

[OPEN Applications or All Churches]
[HOVER status — do not delete or break data]

Public church registration
can feed an application queue.

Operators provision tenants and hierarchy
instead of hand-building databases.

[OPEN Feature Registry]
[POINT at flags — do not toggle]

Features are switched deliberately per church —
so a pilot congregation can gain
assets or payroll when ready.

[OPEN Audit Log or Operations Health]

Platform actions are audited.
Operations health keeps the runbook honest —
because church finance
can’t afford silent failure.

[RETURN to institution Dashboard or closing slide]

That’s the full arc —
congregation,
to conference,
to platform.

---

## 09:30  CLOSING

[HOLD Mission Control KPIs and Action Queue — or closing slide]
[STOP moving the mouse]

ChurchHub gives executives visibility,
pastors a pastoral queue,
treasurers accountable books,
and administrators clean membership and access control —
in one secure system.

[PAUSE]

Recommended next step:
a guided pilot on your conference hierarchy,
with your chart of accounts,
and two local churches.

[PAUSE — smile]

I’m happy to go deeper
on remittance,
permissions,
or the member portal.

What matters most to your team?
