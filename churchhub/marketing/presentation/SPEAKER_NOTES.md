## Slide 03 — The Challenge

**Time:** Pitch-only (~45–60s); skip in live demo  

Church administration is enterprise work. Spreadsheets break at scale. Point tools don’t respect hierarchy. Finance mistakes aren’t IT issues — they’re trust issues. And pastoral follow-ups get lost between Sabbath and Tuesday.

ChurchHub exists to replace that fragmentation with one permissioned system.

## Slide 05 — Mission Control

**Time:** 01:00–02:00  

This landing page is Mission Control. Executives and pastors get the pulse immediately — giving this month, remittance position, membership shape — scoped to the church you’re working in.

The Action Queue is the pastor and admin to-do: open visitors, pending transfers, financial approvals waiting for a second pair of eyes.

Treasurers see business date and teller activity — so Sabbath posting isn’t guesswork. Trends show income versus expense over recent months. Pastoral signals — birthdays, meetings, follow-ups — sit in the same place.

## Slide 06 — People

**Time:** 02:00–04:00  

Clerks and pastors search a living directory — always limited to churches they’re authorized to see. A member profile is the single record: identity, status, family, placement — not a spreadsheet tab per department. The journey timeline preserves baptism, transfer, and leadership history.

Visitors: capture who came, who invited them, what’s next — so guests don’t disappear by Tuesday. When they’re ready, conversion carries visit history into membership.

**Click cue:** Giving / Slide 07.

---

## Slide 07 — Stewardship & Treasury

**Time:** 04:00–06:00  

Giving lookup is permission-controlled. Statements show tithe, offerings, and period totals — suitable for counseling or year-end conversations.

At the Sabbath desk, tellers post against the church’s business date — not the laptop clock. Every posting lands on a printable confirmation. Sensitive changes can use maker-checker — one records, another approves.

At conference level, remittance and monthly cut-off roll local faithfulness into organizational accountability.

**Chart note:** Sample tithe vs combined by church.

## Slide 08 — Church Life

**Time:** 07:00–08:00  

Church Life pulls the week together — meetings, birthdays, communications — so the pastoral team shares one calendar truth.

Board work isn’t email archaeology: agenda, minutes, decisions, and attendance live on the meeting record. Announcements publish to the church — and the member portal can surface the same message on phones.

## Slide 09 — Reports & Integrity

**Time:** 06:00–07:00  

One report center — membership, attendance, stewardship, and accounting — filtered by what your role may run.

Pastors and executives answer “How are we doing in stewardship?” in seconds. Exports are ready for board packs — still permission-aware, still tenant-scoped.

For treasurers and auditors: trial balance, income statement, balance sheet — double-entry integrity, not a second set of books.

## Slide 10 — Security, Access & Platform

**Time:** 08:00–09:30  

Least privilege, denomination wall, operator lane.

RBAC: roles, overrides, effective permissions. Tenancy: church scope plus denomination isolation — no cross-tenant leakage. Platform: subscriptions, feature flags, audit, and operations health — because church finance can’t afford silent failure.

Features switch deliberately per church — so a pilot can gain payroll or assets when ready.
