# ChurchHub Enterprise — Executive Summary

**Document type:** Sales kit · Executive briefing  
**Product:** ChurchHub Enterprise — Church Management System (ChMS)  
**Positioning:** Unified membership, treasury, remittance, and church life across multi-level hierarchy — with books you can trust.

---

## The opportunity

Church networks already operate like enterprises: multi-site hierarchy, fiduciary duty over tithe and offerings, pastoral follow-up, and audit expectations. Most still run on **spreadsheets, point tools, or flat single-church software** that cannot enforce scope, remittance discipline, or double-entry integrity at conference scale.

ChurchHub is built for that gap.

---

## What ChurchHub is

A **server-rendered, production-grade Django platform** that delivers:

| Pillar | Outcome |
|--------|---------|
| **Hierarchy** | General Conference → Union → Conference → Zone → District → Local Church |
| **People** | Members, visitors, families, transfers, leadership, church history |
| **Money** | Double-entry books of record, receipts/expenses, giving statements, remittance & settlements |
| **Church life** | Meetings & minutes, announcements, calendar, member portal |
| **Control** | RBAC, church scope, denomination isolation, separate platform operator lane |
| **Insight** | Mission Control dashboard, Action Queue, report center with exports |

Optional modules (feature-gated per tenant): payroll, fixed assets, budgets, advanced ledger UI, contribution campaigns.

---

## Why boards choose ChurchHub

1. **Financial integrity** — Debits = credits; locked approved journals; void via reversal; working-day and period controls.  
2. **Least privilege** — Permission matrix and server-side church scope (not UI-only hiding).  
3. **True multi-tenancy** — Denomination wall + institution apps vs `/platform/` operator console.  
4. **One operating rhythm** — Mission Control for “what to do next,” not chart wallpaper.  
5. **Deploy your way** — Shared cloud, dedicated/VPC, or private data center (PostgreSQL, Redis, Celery, Gunicorn, Nginx).
