### What you get

- **Mission Control** — KPIs, Action Queue, teller console, pastoral pulse  
- **Membership lifecycle** — Directory, visitors, families, transfers, history  
- **Treasury** — Receipts with printable confirmation, approvals, periods, remittance  
- **Church life** — Meetings & minutes, announcements, calendar, portal  
- **Reports** — Stewardship, membership, attendance, accounting exports  
- **Platform** — Denominations, subscriptions, tenant provisioning, audit  

### Why it matters

Financial mistakes are trust issues. ChurchHub keeps **debits equal credits**, scopes data by **role and church**, and separates the **institution workspace** from the **platform operator lane**.
