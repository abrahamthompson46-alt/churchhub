When ChurchHub is the right fit

  • You manage more than one church under a shared hierarchy
  • Treasury must stay auditable across remittance cycles
  • Pastors and clerks need one Action Queue — not five inboxes
  • You may grow into multi-denomination or private hosting

When to keep another tool

  • Single micro-congregation with no finance process yet
  • You only need a public website CMS (ChurchHub is operations software)
  • You already standardized on an ERP and only need a portal — talk to us about scope
