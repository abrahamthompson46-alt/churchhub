Why ChurchHub

Church administration is enterprise work

Membership, tithe, remittance, and pastoral follow-ups cannot live in disconnected spreadsheets. Financial mistakes are trust issues — not IT footnotes.

  • Hierarchy from General Conference through Union, Conference, Zone, District, to local church
  • Double-entry books of record with approvals, periods, and void-by-reversal
  • Role-based access and church scope enforced on the server
  • Mission Control dashboard with Action Queue for pastors and treasurers

Integrity

Books auditors can follow

Debits equal credits. Approved journals stay locked. Corrections use reversing entries. Working days and periods keep Sabbath posting disciplined.

  • Permission-controlled giving statements
  • Remittance policies and settlements across the hierarchy

Security

  • Domain audit trails for finance and access changes
  • Policy-configurable MFA for privileged roles
